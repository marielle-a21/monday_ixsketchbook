These are a few artists who inspire me


Ariana Grande

Ariana Grande's Spotify


I chose Ariana Grande because of her ability to tell heartfelt and personal stories through her music. The way she curates the melody and lyrics so meticulously that it really makes me feel immersed in the music.

It inspires me to see how she uses her skills in the studio since she alway has ideas on new harmonies and ad-libs that truly make the song unique.


DNEG Studios

DNEG Studios' Website


I chose DNEG Studios because of the way they bring imaginative worlds to life through vibrant visuals.

I love the saturation of their color palettes throughout the movie and how cozy it makes me feel. I also really like how you're able to see the brush strokes in the scenes because it makes me realize how simple shapes can create elaborate compositions.


Tuoer_sg

Tuoer_sg's X


I chose Tuoer_sg because of the beauty behind his moody pieces of art. I love how each piece gives a melancholic vibe as it isn't seen as conventional.

He also creates many strange and futuristic settings such as robots in a city, jellyfish in the sky, or giant objects that I think is very different.

When making hyper external links, I learned that "target"_blank"" is used to force the link to open in a new browser, tab, or window. Once I did, everything was working perfect. I decided to be direct, clear, and concise with my wording in my alt text by picking out the details in each image.
