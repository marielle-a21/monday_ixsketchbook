about me

this is me



Hi, my name is Marielle Ann Adecer and I was born in Fairfax, Virginia, but lived in Silver Spring, Maryland for most of my life. I moved to a small town in California called Mammoth Lakes and I've lived there for 6 years.

I'm majoring in Visual Communication Design at San Francisco State University.

I like to do many different types of art such as playing the guitar, singing, drawing, doing nails, and painting. As well as a variety of little arts and crafts.

As I said before, I love to do nails. I like making press ons with fun designs for both me and others, and I always love trying new techniques. I want to be able to create visually pleasing media and designsthat share inspirational messages. I also hope to someday have my own press-on nail business, so I would love to learn how to create websites and advertisements for it as well.

I'm taking this class because it is a required course for my major, as well as I enjoy learning new things, like coding.

I hope to learn how to become efficient in HTML as I think it will be a needed skill in design professions.

No matter the design, I want to be able to create something that means something to people.


In this exercise, I learned how to embed a video into HTML as well as insert a live link. I decided for my video self-portrait to be me singing and playing my guitar. I chose this because even though I'm still learning how to play, it is something I really enjoy practicing. It also lets me express my deep love for music as well as singing does.

In my biography, I decided to include Virginia and Maryland as the places where I'm from. I chose to do this because both of those places are very special to me and I could never just pick one to say where I'm from.

I also decided to include Mammoth Lakes, CA in my biography because I've lived there from ages 12-18. Even though they weren't the best years of my life, I know I've grown a lot mentally there and was faced with difficulties that made me who I am today.

I chose to place my external link under where I mentioned I would like to create visually pleasing media. I did this because I'm constantly changing my social media feed to curate an aesthetic page that represents me.
